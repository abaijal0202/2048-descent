How to play

  • Move: drag your finger across the board to slide the falling number between columns, or use the arrow buttons.
  • Drop: swipe down on the board, tap DROP, or press Space.
  • Merge: equal numbers combine whenever they end up touching — above/below or left/right. Merges chain until nothing else matches.
  • Speed: crossing 512, 1024 and 2048 each add +20% fall speed.
  • 2048: the first 2048 clears the board and locks into the bottom-left corner as a permanent trophy.
  • Delete Row: clears the lowest row and settles everything down. 3 charges, +1 every 30 min.
  • Slow: reduces fall speed for 30 seconds. 3 charges, +1 every 30 min.
