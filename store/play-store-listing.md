## Full description (4000 char limit)

```
Numbers fall one at a time. Equal numbers merge on contact. The stack keeps rising.

2048 Descent takes the falling-block tension of Tetris and the satisfying doubling of 2048, then makes them fight each other. Every tile you drop is a decision: chase a merge now, or build the staircase that pays off in ten moves.

MERGE IN TWO DIRECTIONS
Matching tiles combine when they touch — sideways as well as downward. A single well-placed tile can trigger a chain that collapses half the board. Chains pay progressively more, so a five-deep cascade is worth far more than five separate merges. Learning to build them is the whole game.

STOP TIME AND PLAN
Trapped? Spend a Plan charge. For fifteen seconds gravity switches off completely and the board becomes a classic 2048 grid — swipe in any direction and every tile slides, merging on contact. Park tiles in mid-air, rebuild your stack, set up something enormous.

Then the timer runs out, gravity comes back, and everything you assembled falls at once. That's where the biggest combos in the game come from.

THREE POWERS, ALWAYS FREE
- PLAN — 15 seconds of gravity-free 2048
- DELETE ROW — pick any row and clear it, so you decide what goes
- SLOW — stretch the fall interval for 30 seconds

Three charges each, and one charge regenerates every 30 minutes whether the app is open or not. No ads for extra lives. No paywalled continues.

CLIMB THE TROPHY LADDER
Reach 2048 and the board clears — your trophy locks permanently into the corner and every future merge scores more. Then the target becomes 4096. Then 8192. Each rung speeds the board up, so the game keeps asking harder questions instead of quietly ending after one win.

BUILT TO FEEL RIGHT
- Tiles fall smoothly and pop when they merge
- Haptics scale with combo depth — you feel a big chain building
- The top of the board warns you before the stack reaches the ceiling
- Your run is saved automatically, so a phone call never costs you a game
- Portrait, one-handed, no account, no sign-in

HOW TO PLAY
Drag anywhere on the board to steer the falling tile. Swipe down to drop it fast. Watch the landing outline — it turns solid when landing there will trigger a merge.

The tile always enters in the middle column. Block that column and the run is over, so leaving yourself an exit is as important as scoring.

Simple to start. Genuinely hard to master.
```
`2,187 / 4000`

## Notes for the Play Console form

- **Category:** Games → Puzzle
- **Tags:** puzzle, casual, brain, numbers, merge, blocks
- **Content rating:** expect Everyone — no violence, no user content, no data collection
- **Ads:** none. **In-app purchases:** none — say so in the form, it is a genuine
  differentiator in this category and the description leans on it.
- **Data safety:** the app collects and transmits nothing. Scores, power charges and the
  saved run all live in local SharedPreferences and never leave the device. Declare "No
  data collected".

### One thing to fix before you submit

The description promises "no ads, no paywalled continues", which is true today. The
README already flags that if power charges ever become purchasable, the regeneration
balance has to move server-side — a player can currently refill instantly by changing the
device clock. If monetisation arrives, this copy and that mechanic both need revisiting.
